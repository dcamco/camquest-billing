CAMQUEST · TIME & BILLING

Possibilities: what the system can grow into once the core is running.

Version 1 attacks the two frictions directly: techs not entering time (work going out free) and clients paying late instead of up front. Everything below is a candidate for later, and each item earns its place by capturing more hours or moving cash earlier — never by adding something to maintain.

v1 · Corev2 · Capturev3 · Connectv4 · Revenuev5 · Intelligencev6 · Scale ← MockupDecisions →
The rule for every item here: it ships only when something hurts without it. Order and version numbers are a sequence, not a schedule — items move up when a client asks or a number is wrong, and move down when they don't.

v1 The core — what we build first

In scope for version 1

  • Prepaid blocks (20 h @ $160, 40 h @ $140) and T&M at $180
  • Tech time entry from a phone: date, client, device, on-site/remote, notes; quarter-hour rounding with 1 h on-site / .25 h remote minimums
  • Weekly approval queue with edit, reject-with-reason, and period lock after payroll
  • Double-entry ledger: client block burn, tech pay accrual, unearned → recognized revenue
  • Tech "My pay" view: approved vs. pending, prior deposits
  • Internal / non-billable time and hours-worked vs. hours-billed for payroll compliance
  • QuickBooks Online sync: customers, block invoices, TimeActivity, month-end journal entry, payment webhook
  • Monthly invoice run: T&M, Azure backup per GB, hardware, quarterly RMM with device counts
  • Blocks unlock only when payment posts; work without a paid block bills at T&M
  • Receivables: due / past-due everywhere, aging, automated reminder cadence, card on file / autopay via QBO Payments
  • Client portal: hours, health panel, work history, invoices, pay online, buy a block
  • N-sight nightly sync: device picker, failing-check nudges, portal health, RMM counts
  • Reconciliation gate, audit log, sales-tax tagging, Entra sign-in, nightly backups, CSV export
  • Everything Tami does by hand today — chasing techs for hours, keying pay and block deductions, entering QuickBooks, collecting — handled by the system so her role can retire with her
  • Block terms clients accept in the portal — no expiry, T&M after exhaustion, minimums, payment terms — so the rules are agreed, not assumed
  • Runbook and documentation a future buyer would expect in due diligence

v2 Capture & communication — make time entry disappear, make clients feel informed

The fastest payback after v1: more hours captured from techs, fewer reasons for clients to delay paying.

Teams / text time entry

Techs keep sending time the way they do today — a Teams message or text like "Bayou 1.5 remote, fixed scanner." The app turns it into a draft entry; the tech taps confirm. No new app to open.

High valueMedium effortClaude API · Teams bot

Weekly client digest

Friday email per client: hours used this week, what was done, hours remaining, next invoice due. Kills most disputes before they start and quietly sells the next block.

High valueLow effort

Burn forecast & renewal nudge

"Gulf Coast runs out ~Dec 12 at current pace." Shown to the owner and, at the threshold, sent to the client with the 20/40 offer. Nobody finds out at zero.

High valueLow effort

Request intake in the portal

Not a ticketing system — a form: what's wrong, how urgent. Becomes a draft entry the tech picks up, so every entry has a reference the client recognizes.

Medium valueLow effort

Approval sanity flags

Before Scott approves: duplicate-looking entries, unusual durations for that tech, a note naming a different client, after-hours entries with no alert behind them. Yellow line, human decides.

Medium valueLow effort

Mileage on on-site visits

A per-visit mileage field that flows to payroll as a reimbursement line instead of a spreadsheet someone forgets.

Medium valueLow effort

v3 Connect — every number comes from its source

Each integration replaces a number someone types with one the system reads.

Microsoft 365 tenant signals

Read-only Graph access per client tenant: licensed accounts with no sign-in in 90 days, users without MFA, mailboxes near quota, subscription renewal dates. Feeds suggestions and the health panel.

High valueMedium effortGraph app registration per tenant

Microsoft license resale (CSP)

If Camquest resells M365, Partner Center seat counts become a monthly invoice line per client. Same pattern as RMM device counts.

High valueMedium effortPartner Center API

Azure Cost Management per client

Backup GB and any other per-client Azure spend pulled nightly, so the storage line is exact and margin includes real cost.

Medium valueLow effortAzure subscription tagging

Cove backup (when adopted)

If backup moves to N-able Cove, usage rides the existing N-able sync — no new integration.

Medium valueLow effortCove

Vendor bills matched to clients

N-able, Microsoft and Azure invoices split by client, so margin per client is revenue minus tech pay minus what Camquest actually pays.

Medium valueMedium effortQBO Bills + classes

Hardware → RMM asset link

When a hardware line is billed, the app watches for the new device at that client in N-sight and links invoice to asset. Answers "when did we put that switch in?" instantly.

Low valueLow effort

v4 Revenue — bill everything Camquest sells

cqsys.com lists cloud, disaster recovery, procurement, web design, VoIP and Power Automate. Each becomes a line the engine already knows how to bill.

Project work with milestones

Migrations and website builds as fixed-price projects: deposit, progress, final. Distinct from blocks so techs don't log project time against a client's prepaid hours.

High valueMedium effort

Renewals tracker

Domains, SSL, hosting plans with dates → auto-invoice 30 days out → the existing reminder cadence. Lapsed domains are how small IT shops lose clients.

High valueLow effort

VoIP per-seat pass-through

Monthly line per client from the VoIP provider's seat count.

Medium valueLow effortProvider API or monthly import

Quote → block / hardware with e-sign

Portal quotes the client accepts in one click; acceptance creates the QBO invoice. Worth it once block and hardware sales are frequent.

Medium valueMedium effort

Buyer-ready reporting

High valueLow effort

CPA year-end pack

Medium valueLow effort

v5 Intelligence — the system suggests, people decide

One firm rule: AI never writes a posting. It reads, flags, drafts and suggests; a human approves anything touching hours, money or a client.

Engagement suggestions for techs

Nightly: "Heights runs out Dec 12 — mention the 40-hr block Thursday." "Bayou pays for 3 unused M365 seats — offer to clean up." "No entries at Heights in 23 days — a 15-minute check-in." Accept / snooze / not relevant, with evidence attached.

High valueMedium effortv3 signals

Reconciliation explainer

When the nightly check finds a mismatch, the model reads both sides and says why in plain English: "QBO has a manual journal entry dated 11/3 for $560 that isn't in the ledger."

High valueLow effort

Billing anomaly digest

Device count jumps overnight, a client's backup GB doubles, a block burns 30% faster than its history, a client not billed in 90 days. One Monday email to the owner.

Medium valueLow effort

Reminder tone per client

Dunning emails drafted in the right register — first-time slip vs. chronic — from the client's payment history. Template approved once; the model fills it.

Medium valueLow effort

Month-end narrative

"October: $18.4k recognized, $3.2k deferred, AR aging stable, margin 41%, Gulf Coast is 62% of T&M — concentration risk." The CPA pack with a cover note.

Medium valueLow effort

Asset age & refresh proposals

From N-sight: workstations past OS end-of-support, servers past warranty. Drafts a refresh quote with the hardware line ready.

Medium valueMedium effort

v6 Scale — when there are more clients, techs, or both

Multi-contact portal with roles

Owner sees invoices and pays; office manager sees hours only.

Medium valueLow effort

One-tap satisfaction

"How was this week? 👍 👎" in the digest. Two clicks of data per client per week; a quarter's trend shows who is about to leave.

Medium valueLow effortv2 digest
