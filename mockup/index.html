Camquest Systems, LLC · cqsys.com·Time-block billing & payroll·For review·Nov 7, 2026

Get paid up front, capture every hour, stop chasing: one ledger entry burns the client's prepaid block, accrues the tech's pay, and queues the QuickBooks record.

The two frictions this removes: techs not entering time (work going out free) and clients drifting from prepaid blocks to T&M paid late after hounding. This is a working slice of the proposed system. Switch who you are (a tech, Drew, or a client) and the scenario above the frame, then use it like the real thing: log time and watch the rounding rules apply, approve the queue and watch balances and pay move, run month-end, or buy a block from the portal.

Why: techs weren't entering time, clients were paying late instead of up front, and Tami was doing every deduction, pay entry, QuickBooks line and collection call by hand. This replaces all of it so she can retire and the business runs on a system a buyer could take over. Design rule: the block discount is the reward for paying first — hours unlock when payment posts, work without a paid block bills at T&M, and techs are paid only on logged, approved time. Decided so far: QuickBooks Online (Plus + Payroll Core) replaces Desktop before build; hosted on Azure with Entra sign-in; the app's ledger is the source of truth and QBO is the books. RMM is N-able N-sight, synced nightly into the device picker, portal health panel and RMM invoice counts; backup is native Azure (not Cove). Open: what happens when a block runs out, who approves, RMM fee structure, and whether salaried time burns client blocks. Pick those in Decisions.

The six steps, in one place

1Sell a block20 hrs @ $160 or 40 hrs @ $140. App creates a QBO Invoice with a Prepaid Support Block item mapped to Unearned Revenue (liability). Cash in, no revenue yet.
2Tech logs timeDuration rounds up to .25. On-site floors at 1.0 hr, remote at .25. Entry sits as Submitted until approved.
3ApproveOldest block debited at its locked rate. Tech accrues billed hrs × their rate (Mark 85, Rick 50, Robert 60). One TimeActivity queued to QBO.
4Month endPer client: hours consumed × block rate moves Unearned Revenue → Service Revenue (journal entry). T&M hours, Azure backup GB, hardware and quarterly RMM go on one Invoice.
5PayrollPay-period hours already sit in QBO as TimeActivity. Rate lives on the QBO Employee. Drew clicks Run Payroll; direct deposit unchanged.
6ReconcileBlock liability in app vs. Unearned Revenue in QBO; pay accrual vs. payroll run. Both match or the dashboard goes red. This is what lets the bookkeeper retire.

Core tables

Client (QBO id, T&M rate, minimums, low-balance threshold) · Block (hours, rate, remaining, QBO invoice id) · Employee (Entra id, pay type, $/hr, QBO id) · TimeEntry (status draft → submitted → approved → synced) · LedgerTxn (immutable debit/credit per block) · Charge (storage GB, hardware, RMM) · SyncLog (every QBO call and result).

Compliance flag, not a blocker. Paying W-2 non-exempt techs only on billed hours is piece-rate pay. Non-billable hours worked still have to clear minimum wage and overtime applies past 40 hours worked. A common structure is a small hourly base plus a per-billed-hour commission. Confirm with your payroll/HR advisor before the formula is locked in; the ledger math is the same either way.

3 · Alternatives considered

Custom app (this mockup)

Exactly your rules, your portal, your QBO mapping. Azure cost ~$60–120/mo. You own the code; maintainable from Claude Code. Cost is build time and you are the vendor.

MSP PSA tool with QBO sync

Several PSAs do prepaid-hour blocks, ticketing and QBO sync out of the box, per-tech per month. Faster start; you'd bend your pay-per-quarter-hour rule and portal to their model, and per-client block rates are often awkward.

QBO alone + spreadsheets

QBO can track retainers and timesheets natively. No portal, no automatic rounding/minimums, no pay accrual, and someone still keys the deductions. This is roughly what the bookkeeper does today.

Stay on QB Desktop

Integration via the QBXML SDK / Web Connector: Windows-only, polling, breaks on upgrades. Not recommended if the Online move is happening anyway.

Accrual vs. cash treatment of blocks

The mock posts block sales to a liability and recognizes revenue as hours burn (accrual). If your CPA files you cash-basis and doesn't want the liability account, the item flips to revenue at sale and the month-end journal entry is skipped. Ten-minute question for the CPA; the app supports both.
