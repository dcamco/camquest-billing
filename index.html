CAMQUEST · TIME & BILLING
A proposal for Scott · Camquest Systems

Get paid up front. Capture every hour. Stop chasing.

Camquest sells discounted blocks of support hours that are meant to be prepaid. In practice, techs don’t always enter their time, so work goes out free — and clients drift onto time-and-materials, paying in chunks at the end after repeated follow-up. This proposal is a system that removes both frictions and keeps cash flowing: blocks that only exist once they’re paid, time entry that takes thirty seconds on a phone, and reminders that run themselves. It also takes over everything Tami does by hand today — so she can retire — and leaves behind a documented, automated business that a future buyer would value.
